Our Story

About Zion Trybe

A place where FAITH meets FAMILY — raising people who carry the presence of God into every corner of Lagos and beyond.

Who We Are

God's Dwelling Place
in the Heart of Lagos

Zion Trybe is a Spirit-filled, family-centered church where everyday people meet a real and present God.

We exist to help you encounter God, grow in authentic faith, and find a family that walks with you through every season. We are not about religion or perfection — we are about presence, transformation, and love.

From our El-Ezer teachings to our seasons of Harvest and Thanksgiving, everything we do points back to one truth: surely the presence of the Lord is in this place.

🔥

Our Mission

To raise a generation that carries the presence of God — winning souls, building disciples, and loving people into wholeness.

👁️

Our Vision

To be God's dwelling place in Lagos: a thriving family where faith is alive, families are healed, and destinies are fulfilled.

❤️

Our Values

Presence over performance. Family over formality. The Word as our foundation, and love as our language.

Our Leadership

Pastor Yomi & Pastor Tosin Hassan

Leading Zion Trybe together with faith, fire, and a father-and-mother's heart for the family.

Pastor Yomi Hassan

Lead Pastor

Pastor Yomi Hassan is the visionary and lead pastor of Zion Trybe. A passionate teacher and prophetic voice, he is known for messages that are both deep and practical — like the El-Ezer series, drawn from Exodus 18:4, reminding us that God is our ever-present Help.

His heart is to see ordinary believers walk in extraordinary intimacy with God and step boldly into their God-given purpose.

He pastors Zion Trybe alongside his wife and ministry partner, Co-Pastor Tosin Yomi Hassan, whose warmth, wisdom, and care nurture the church into a true family. Together they lead with humility, joy, and an unwavering commitment to God's presence.
