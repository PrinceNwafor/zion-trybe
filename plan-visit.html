We'd Love to Meet You

Plan Your Visit

Your first Sunday should feel easy and warm. Here's everything you need to know before you come.

What to Expect

1
Arrive & Be Welcomed
A friendly greeter will meet you at the door, help you settle in, and answer any questions.
2
Worship & the Word
Expect heartfelt worship and a practical, life-giving message. Services run about 2 hours.
3
Your Kids Are Cared For
Our Children's Ministry offers a safe, fun space so you can engage fully in the service.
4
Let's Connect
After service, come say hello. We'd love to meet you and help you find your place in the family.

Service Times & Location

Sundays: 8:30am WAT

Midweek: Wednesdays 6:00pm · Prayer: Fridays 6:00pm

Address: 20 Adewusi Street, Fadeyi, Ikorodu Road, Lagos, Nigeria
