Your Pastors

Meet Pastor Yomi & Pastor Tosin

Leading Zion Trybe with a heart for God's presence, sound teaching, and a genuine love for people.

Pastor Yomi Hassan

Lead Pastor

Pastor Yomi Hassan is a passionate teacher of the Word and a prophetic voice raising a generation that carries the presence of God. His ministry blends depth, fire, and warmth — helping everyday people encounter a real God who is El-Ezer, our ever-present Help.

He serves alongside his wife and partner in ministry, Co-Pastor Tosin Yomi Hassan, whose grace and care shape Zion Trybe into the loving family it is today.
