Our Ministries

There's a place for everyone to belong, grow, and serve at Zion Trybe. Find your people and your purpose.

🙏

Prayer (Trybe Altar)

Our engine room. We gather every Friday to seek God's face, intercede for one another, and contend for breakthrough.

🎶

Worship & Creative Arts

Leading the family into God's presence through music, sound, and creativity that glorifies God.

🔥

Youth & Young Adults

Raising a fearless generation rooted in the Word, building genuine friendships and bold faith.

👶

Children's Ministry

A safe, fun, faith-filled space where children learn to love God and discover who He made them to be.

🤝

Hospitality & Ushering

The first smile you see. Our team makes sure every guest feels seen, welcomed, and at home.

📖

Discipleship & Growth

Helping every member grow from guest to family to disciple through teaching, mentoring, and community.

Joining a team is one of the fastest ways to find family at Zion Trybe. Tell us where you'd love to plug in.
